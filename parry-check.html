Valheim Parry & Stagger Calculator

Which hits your shield actually stops, at your loadout and difficulty. A parry breaks when the damage that gets past your shield is 40% of your max health or more, and max health drains as food runs out.

Which enemies your weapon staggers, and in how many hits. An enemy staggers when the stagger damage it has taken reaches its stagger limit (max health × its stagger factor); the bar drains to zero over 5 seconds.

Your character

Fills in your shield, block skill, armor and foods with their exact time left. In game press F5 and type save, then choose your characters folder and the most recently saved character is loaded. Steam cloud saves: Steam\userdata\<id>\892970\remote\characters. Local saves: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\IronGate\Valheim\characters_local. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded, even if your browser's prompt says "upload".

            Numbers come from the game files (build 2026-10-30): enemy weapon damage and attack multipliers, shield block power, food values, and the game's own block, stagger and armor formulas. Worst case is assumed: enemy damage roll at its maximum, your stagger bar empty, full health and stamina, all three foods at the same time left.
            Resistances from armor, set bonuses, meads, forsaken powers and being wet are applied to the damage that lands on you. Not modelled: other status effects (some change parry bonus or stagger), NG+ world levels, cold debuffs. Fire and poison are shown as damage over time and don't count toward the one-shot check. Biomes marked "?" are a best guess for creatures that aren't in the normal spawn tables.
